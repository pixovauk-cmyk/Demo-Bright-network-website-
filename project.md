# BrightPeak — Project Analysis

## Company Overview
**Name:** BrightPeak Group / BrightPeak Apprenticeships
**URL:** https://apps.brightpeakgroup.com/
**Tagline:** "Build a team that grows with your business"
**Type:** Government-funded apprenticeship training provider (UK)

## What They Do
End-to-end apprenticeship delivery — from recruitment through qualification completion.
Fully funded by the UK government (no cost to employer in most cases).

## Key Stats
- 30+ years in apprenticeships
- Ofsted "Good" rating
- 85% average success rate
- 100% government-funded
- Sub-brands: Workpays, WS Training, Orangebox

## Target Audiences

### Employers
- HR/L&D managers wanting skilled staff
- Businesses in financial services, hospitality, retail, manufacturing, charity, public sector
- Companies wanting to grow talent pipelines without recruitment cost

### Learners
- Job seekers wanting vocational qualifications
- Employees upskilling within current employer
- Career changers entering finance, tech, business roles

## Delivery Methods
1. In-person — purpose-built training facilities
2. Online — live tutor-led remote sessions
3. On-site — trainer visits the workplace

## Customer Journey

### Employer Journey
1. Discovery (organic/referral/ad)
2. Free Skills Audit / Training Needs Analysis
3. Free Discovery Call
4. Programme co-design
5. BrightPeak handles recruitment
6. Apprentice starts, BrightPeak manages compliance + delivery
7. Real-time progress tracking via employer portal
8. Qualification achievement / renewal

### Learner Journey
1. Browse vacancies or arrive via employer
2. Apply / register interest
3. CV Builder tool
4. Interview prep
5. Apprenticeship starts
6. Training sessions (online + in-person)
7. Off-the-job training hours tracked
8. End-point assessment
9. Qualification achieved
